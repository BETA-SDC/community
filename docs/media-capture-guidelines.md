## Use Cases

This guideline is intended for small organizations creating media for website display, event documentation, and non-professional video editing. The goal is to keep materials clear, consistent, easy to organize, and reusable, without aiming for professional film production standards.

## General Requirements

| Item | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | **Shoot horizontally** | Horizontal materials are preferred for website display and standard video editing |
| Aspect ratio | **16:9** | Keep videos and photos consistent, reducing the need for extra cropping later |
| File format | **Common, widely supported formats** | Use the device's default format for videos, and JPEG for photos |
| Stability | **Keep the camera as stable as possible** | Avoid fast panning, frequent zooming, and walking while shooting |
| Lighting | **Prefer bright environments** | Avoid backlight, very dark scenes, or strong colored lighting that makes people hard to see |
| Composition | **Keep the subject complete and the background clean** | Leave space around people's heads and body edges, and avoid placing important content too close to the edge |

## Video

### Device: DJI Pocket 3

| Parameter | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **16:9** | Common horizontal format; can be cropped for vertical use later |
| Resolution | **4K** | Leaves room for cropping and stabilization |
| Frame rate | **30fps** for daily use / **60fps** for slow motion | 30fps is generally smooth; 60fps works better when footage needs to be slowed down |

### Shooting Notes

- Keep each shot around **5-10 seconds** when possible. Avoid stopping immediately after starting.
- When filming people speaking, presenting, or interacting, hold the frame steady for **1-2 seconds** before moving.
- Avoid digital zoom when possible. Move closer or recompose the shot instead.
- For speeches, interviews, or explanations, stay close to the sound source and avoid recording from far behind the crowd.
- For important scenes, capture a wide shot, a medium shot, and a detail shot when possible. This gives editors more usable options.

## Photos

### Device: Camera

| Parameter | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **16:9** | Matches the video format and is easier to use directly in editing |
| Resolution | **Default/standard resolution is enough** | Suitable for website display and regular editing, while avoiding unnecessarily large files |
| Format | **JPEG** | Widely supported and recognized by editing software |

> Note: 16:9 camera photos are usually cropped from the sensor's native aspect ratio. Leave extra space at the top and bottom while shooting to avoid cutting off heads, feet, or the tops of buildings.

### Phone as Backup

| Parameter | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **16:9** | Matches the video format and reduces additional cropping before editing |
| Pixels | **Default pixel setting is enough** | Suitable for website display and regular editing, while avoiding large files and slow transfers |
| Format (iPhone) | **Most Compatible (JPEG)** | Widely supported and recognized by editing software |
| Format (Android) | **JPEG** | Widely supported and recognized by editing software |

### Shooting Notes

- Take at least **2-3 photos** of each important scene, so there are options if someone blinks, the focus is off, or the expression is not ideal.
- For group photos, make sure faces are clear and not blocked. Avoid only capturing backs or distant views.
- When photographing boards, posters, screens, or whiteboards, shoot as straight-on as possible to avoid distortion and reflections.
- For environment photos, include recognizable location features such as entrances, event backdrops, work scenes, or key equipment.
- Avoid extra beauty filters, stylized filters, or heavy color grading, so materials from different devices remain visually consistent.

## Content Safety

- Before photographing screens, whiteboards, documents, chat groups, or name badges, check that no sensitive information is visible.
- For minors, guests, external partners, or anyone who may not be suitable for public display, confirm permission before publishing.
- Avoid uploading materials that are clearly unsuitable for website display, such as awkward expressions, inappropriate poses, cluttered scenes, or unrelated ads in the background.
- Do not over-retouch photos or add strong filters. Keep the organization's image authentic, clear, and consistent.

## Material Delivery

| Item | Recommendation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Selection | **Remove clearly unusable materials first** | Examples include severe blur, accidental shots, closed eyes, or very dark images |
| Retention | **Keep multiple photos/videos for important scenes** | Do not keep only one option; this makes it easier to choose better material later |
| Naming | **Organize folders by date and event** | For example: `2026-09-16-activity-name` |
| Upload | **Upload original files when possible** | Avoid repeated compression through chat apps before delivery |
| Notes | **Add a short text description** | Include the event name, shooting time, location, and key people to make materials easier to find later |
