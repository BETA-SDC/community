## Document Taxonomy

Public materials are kept under `docs/`:

- `docs/regulations/`: formal rules and requirements.
- `docs/procedures/`: repeatable workflows and operating procedures.
- `docs/resources/`: tools, tutorials, background information, and references.

The Wiki index organizes entry points by task; the linked source documents remain authoritative.
